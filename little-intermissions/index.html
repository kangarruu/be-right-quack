ON YOUR DESKTOP

The duck can leave this panel.

A small floating duck for your screen. It naps above the Dock, wanders now and then, and says something silly once in a while. After a long stretch of work it waves you over. Click it and it covers your desk for five minutes. Ignore it and it backs off. Drag it anywhere.

The floating duck needs macOS 13 or later and the Xcode command line tools. It only reads how long it has been since your last key or mouse movement, not the keys or your work. No sound. Its menu briefly takes focus. Right-click for a focus sprint, Convince me…, break timing, quiet time, or to send it home. Keyboard controls are available through node /path/to/little-intermissions/duck-cli.mjs help. See the included README for installation and the optional terminal command; installing this extension does not install a global duck command.
